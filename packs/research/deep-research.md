# Purpose

Produce a decision-useful synthesis whose claims can be traced, challenged,
and updated. This is not a substitute for licensed professional judgment.

# Inputs

- `QUESTION`, `AUDIENCE`, `CONSTRAINTS`, `KNOWN_CONTEXT`, `DONE_BAR`

# Template

```text
Research QUESTION for AUDIENCE under CONSTRAINTS.

Before searching, produce a priority ledger containing the exact decision,
hard constraints, known context, unknowns, and done bar. Decompose the question
into claims that can be checked independently.

For every material claim, distinguish observation, source report, inference,
and hypothesis; prefer primary and current sources; record date, scope, and
limitations; identify a plausible rival explanation; and state evidence that
would falsify or materially weaken it.

Build the synthesis around the decision, not discovery order. Resolve source
conflicts explicitly. For quantitative evidence, name the population,
comparison, uncertainty, and whether a calculation was actually performed.
Never label prose as Bayesian, causal, or statistical analysis without the
required model and data.

Run an adversarial pass for missing stakeholders, selection bias, confounding,
base-rate neglect, temporal mismatch, and unsupported certainty. Revise once
when it finds a material defect; otherwise stop.
```

# Output contract

Decision summary; claim-evidence table with confidence and dates; rival
explanations and falsifiers; recommended action or experiment; limitations;
and sources linked at the point of use.

# Stop and escalate

Stop when every decision-critical claim has evidence or an explicit unknown.
Escalate rather than guess when a missing fact could reverse the decision.

# Failure modes

Source list without synthesis; citations that do not support their claim;
popularity treated as evidence; hidden disagreement; recommendations not
entailed by the evidence.
